What this project demonstrates

  • Basic BGP concepts, autonomous systems, and the difference between eBGP and iBGP.
  • eBGP peering between AS 1 and AS 2 across the 10.100.100.0/24 backbone/transit network.
  • iBGP peering between P1R1 and P1R2 inside AS 1.
  • BGP network advertisements and verification with show ip bgp, show ip bgp summary, and routing-table checks.
  • End-to-end reachability testing after BGP route exchange is established.
