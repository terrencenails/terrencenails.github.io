I'm Terrence Nails — an Army veteran (7 years), Senior Systems Engineer at TBM Technology Consulting, and a graduate with a B.S. in Computer Networks & Cybersecurity from the University of Maryland. Starting Spring 2027, I'll be pursuing a Master's in Engineering Management with a concentration in Technical Leadership at Johns Hopkins.

My path into IT actually started before any of that — working as a sales rep at Cricket Wireless, where customers started coming to me specifically to get their phones fixed. Diagnosing what was wrong and solving it myself was more satisfying than sales ever was. One customer even told me I should go into IT, since I was clearly better at fixing the problem than selling around it — advice that stuck, and pushed me toward my first certification, CompTIA A+.

My day-to-day now is hands-on and highly technical, so I built this portfolio to document my own journey — the designs, the failures, and the processes behind them, written down as I go rather than after the fact. When I'm not breaking and fixing things, you'll find me training Muay Thai, at the gym, traveling (12+ countries and counting), or barbecuing.

Whether it was a cracked phone screen or a broken IAM policy, I've always been the one who wants to open it up and figure out why.
